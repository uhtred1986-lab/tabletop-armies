## What people praise

- Free, community-maintained tools with fast points updates.
- A rules and card browser inside the builder, and flags on illegal lists.
- At the table: timing charts, condition reference, dice calculator (Cerebro); phase-aware CP/VP scoring (Tabletop Battles).
- Simple paint-stage columns with a % done (Pile of Potential).

## Common complaints

- The official AoS app hides lists behind a subscription and has no battle tracker.
- Tools get abandoned (BattleScribe, PointBreakSW); card data has errors.
- Every game has its own siloed builder; collection, paint and lists never connect.
- Offline support is rarely documented; tournament apps lock history behind subscriptions.

## Gaps Tabletop Armies fills

1. **Storage places** per unit and per model. No app found does this.
2. A list builder that knows **what you own** and its paint stage.
3. **One play engine** for all five games, **both sides on one tablet**, with undo and a full log.
4. **Private stats** across all games without a tournament platform.
5. **Photos** tied to unit, model, paint stage and game.
6. **Campaigns** across games (generic) plus AoS Path to Glory.

Not copied on purpose (Heyhey, 5 Nov 2026): paint inventory and recipes, barcode scanning, list QR codes.
