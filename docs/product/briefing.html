Gaps Muster fills

  • Storage places per unit and per model. No app found does this.
  • Lists that know your shelf: "only what I own", with each unit's paint stage.
  • One play engine for five games, both sides on one tablet, undo on every action, a full log.
  • Private stats across all games, with no tournament platform or subscription.
  • Photos tied to unit, model, paint stage and game.
  • Campaigns for any game, plus AoS Path to Glory.

What people complain about elsewhere, and Muster avoids: lists behind a subscription, abandoned apps, card data errors with no way to fix them (Muster has overrides), siloed tools per game.

    Look and feel

    Design suggestions

    Each game is a world: the same layout and buttons, its own colours, display face, shapes and ground texture. The swatches below come straight from the design tokens.

    Navigation on each device

    Laptop · 1440 × 900

    208 px text rail: Collection, Places, Lists, Play, Paint, Stats, Campaigns, Data packs. Up to three panes beside it. Dialogs are centred.

    Tablet · 1180 × 820

    88 px icon rail with labels under the icons. Two panes, or three columns in play (phases, units of both sides, scoreboard). Dialogs become bottom sheets.

    Phone · 390 × 844

    Bottom tab bar: Collection, Lists, Play, Paint, More. One pane. In play the phases become a top strip, the two sides switch with a segmented control and the scoreboard is a sheet.

    Suggestions for the new features

    Place picker

    One bottom sheet for every move: places listed with a count of models, the current one marked, "New place" at the bottom. Picking a place moves the whole unit at once and offers Undo.

    Split sheet

    Opens from the unit's place row. A checkbox per model, "Select all", then a place. The unit row then reads "Split · 2 places" in muted ink, never a warning colour, because a split is normal.

    Two cards side by side

    Imported AMG units show Muster's own drawn card (numbers in the game's world) next to the imported card image. The image is labelled "Imported · private" with its source and date, so it is never mistaken for Muster's design.

    Opponent in the same colour logic

    Your side uses the accent stripe; the opponent uses accent-2. Both get the same trackers. The one accent thing on a play screen stays the current phase.

    Photos as a timeline

    A unit's photos read left to right by paint stage, so progress shows at a glance. Model photos sit under the model; the unit shows the newest.

    Stats that explain themselves

    Every number names what it counts ("7 games · Shatterpoint · since March"). Tapping it opens the games behind it. Nothing is stored; all of it is counted from the game log.
